Welcome

One sandbox, every ability

230 castable abilities sorted into 23 elements, all running in a single scene with one character and a set of target dummies.

Pick one, cast it, tune it in the VFX editor, then copy its settings as a preset for your own Three.js game.

This quick tour takes under a minute. You can reopen it any time from Guide.

Find and cast

Tap a card to cast it

  • SpellbookEvery ability is a card in the spellbook (below the stage on a phone). Tapping one selects it and casts straight away.
  • Step through‹ Prev and Next › walk the current filter. On a keyboard use [ and ].
  • Search & filterSearch by name or idea, filter by element, type or aim, and switch between cards and the full table. Grouping stays by element.
  • ProgressA green dot marks every ability you have tried. The header keeps the tally.
Move and aim

Walk the caster around

  • KeyboardClick the stage, then W A S D to walk, Shift to run, Space to dash.
  • TouchUse the on-screen stick to walk, drag the right side of the stage to look around, and tap to cast.
  • Aim it yourselfArm an ability, then aim inside the stage and click or tap to release it. On a phone, open More under the stage for Arm, Auto-cast, the gauntlet and the rest.
  • GauntletRuns every ability in the current filter, one after another — a fast way to see them all.
Tune and take it with you

Copy a preset into your game

  • VFX editorOpens a live panel for colours, timing, particles and more. Changes show on the next cast.
  • Preset JSONShows the full settings block for the selected ability, including your edits. Copy preset puts it on your clipboard.
  • In your gameCopy the preset JSON and apply it with applySettings({ : preset.settings }). You can also paste edited JSON back here.
  • Start overReset to shipped defaults undoes every edit for that ability.
