Void · zone · range 2–16 m (captured at ≈ 11.8 m) · zone radius 3.4 m · ≈ 4.4 s on screen (10 m cast, defaults: 0.3 charge + 0.45 flight + 2.7 assessment and call + 0.8 exit)

## Read in one line
A black square-holed coin is minted in the hand and lobbed at the target; it slots into the floor and the floor tears up into a solid obsidian gravity well the width of the zone, coins rolling the spiral down into its throat while the rim's ledger fills quarter by quarter and the well ratchets round — then the enemy that paid the most is seized, carried round the well and swallowed, and the well folds back into its hole.

## Signature

## Gameplay
- `taxTicks` (4) assessments at `assessDelay + k·taxInterval` after landing. Each levies every enemy inside `zoneRadius` (≤ 8 payers per cast): through the combat binding a UCSM `status` (void → `hollowed`, intensity `taxStatus × levies` ≤ 3, `taxDuration`); on sandbox dummies a `corrode` step (`taxCorrode` per levy). A payer keeps its count if it leaves; a newcomer starts at zero.
- **Eligible construct** = an enemy still standing that has paid at least `eligibleLevies` (3) assessments. At the call (`consumeDelay` after the last assessment) the eligible constructs are ranked by levies paid, then by distance from the throat at their last levy, then by who paid first, and the top `maxConsume` (1, ≤ 3) are consumed: one UCSM `hit` (`consumeDamage` 3, ≤ 10, `hitForce` from `impactForce`, source placed beyond the body so the knock is *inward*), or `Dummy.kill` with the same inward knock. The fallen body is then carried into the throat and consumed (presentation). Everyone else has only paid.
- Interpretation: the sandbox has no construct system (the expansion adapter reports `constructs: false`, and other casts' worldshapes/regions cannot be removed through it), so "construct" is read as the enemy constructs on the field — the target dummies and UCSM enemy actors — and "eligible" as having been assessed enough times to owe the tax in full. `handlesOwnHits` is true; hit timing is independent of reduced motion (which stills the turn, the ratchet's overshoot and the lens).
