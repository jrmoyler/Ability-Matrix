# Expansion combat adapter

New native expansion casts can bind `ctx.expansionCombat.bind('matrix-expansion/<slug>', 'caster')` at spawn time, after the sandbox combat adapter has been installed. A binding fixes its UID, owner and team attribution: callers cannot impersonate another owner by passing those fields to `emit`.

- `on(type, callback)` subscribes to a committed UCSM event; it returns `{ok, unsubscribe}` or `{ok:false, reason}`. Always unsubscribe on expiry, cancellation and disposal. Delivery is synchronous and ordered after the event's combat effects; payloads are detached and deeply frozen. An extension throwing in a callback does not break the combat tick. The shared subscription budget defaults to 64.
- `emit(event)` returns `{ok:true,eventId}` or `{ok:false,reason}`. Events are queued for the next simulation advance and are never inserted into an already-extracted tick. The shared expansion budget defaults to 24 emissions per tick and the UCSM queue cap also applies. The adapter validates element, target IDs, bounded radius, positions and numerical fields, and refuses malformed data. `hit`/`status` require target IDs; `spawn`/`terrain-impact` require a disc (`{kind:'disc',x,z,r}`) or capsule (`{kind:'capsule',ax,az,bx,bz,r}`). Optional fields include `damage`, `intensity`, `durationTicks`, `tags`, and `castId`.
- `getActor(id)` returns `{ok,actor}` or a refusal. `queryActors({position, radius, relation})` returns `{ok,actors}` for `ally`, `enemy`, or `any`; snapshots are read-only, sorted by ID, and limited to 32 live actors. `positionAt(id,ticksAgo)` returns a detached position or null; `.tick`, `.tickRate`, and `.ticksFor(seconds)` expose fixed-step timing.
- `queryRegions({position,radius})` returns up to 48 active elemental-region snapshots, and `isBlocked(from,to)` checks worldshape line-of-sight if that manager is available. These are read-only queries, not a terrain relocation API.
- `queryWorldshapes({position,radius})` returns active worldshape construct snapshots. `swapOwnedWorldshapes(firstId,secondId)` atomically swaps two active worldshapes owned by the caster, preserving their timers and any still-existing elemental imprints and moving their collision and scene positions. It enforces arena/range/overlap and actor-occupancy checks and refuses during an active tick, while linked weaves exist, or while affected terrain events are queued. This is not an arbitrary environmental terrain exchange, and `terrainRelocation` remains false.
- `capabilities` explicitly reports unsupported projectile queries/redirects, force regions, damage redirection, construct creation and mass manipulation. `queryProjectiles()` returns an unsupported refusal. Do not mark a move live if it depends on an unavailable system.

The existing sandbox adapter reports legacy `cast-start` events, landed terrain footprints, and successful dummy knockdowns/freezes. It does **not** report every hit attempt, arbitrary enemy attacks, projectile trajectories or blocked impulse. A move depending on those signals needs a real host integration before it can be declared implemented. The existing 230 casts do not use this facade, and the adapter's idle observer does not modify their rules.
